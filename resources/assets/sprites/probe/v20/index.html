Room animation test: seven rooms

Animated "on" states for seven rooms, made from the approved renders. Off and broken rooms keep their static renders.

How: PixelLab animate_image_pixminimax, 8 frames per part (1 generation each). Rooms whose PixelLab links had expired were sent as a 64×64 or 128×64 crop around the moving part. Each part has a hand-placed mask (the red areas below); only pixels inside it come from the animation and everything else is the original render, so nothing else in the room can move.

Protein vats (redone): the mask is now just the three green glass portholes. The motion is swirling culture and rising bubbles; it plays forward and back.

Presidential suite: the fire flickers inside the fireplace opening, and the chandelier's candle flames flicker. The model would not sway the chandelier; the flames alone read well.

Main generator: the flywheel spins and the gauge needles on the control cabinet twitch. Smelter: the furnace fire roars. Canteen: the pot simmers and steam rises into the hood. Purifier: green liquid bubbles up the sight tube and the pressure gauge twitches. Duct fan: unchanged from the first test.

Cost: 7 animations this round, 7 generations.
