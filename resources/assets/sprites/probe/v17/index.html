Findings

  • Single-slot rooms need a much higher guide strength to stay flat: 75 works for 128 and 192 px, but a 64×96 room is tall and narrow, and the model reads it as a corridor or a box seen from the front. 120–160 holds, at some loss of richness.
  • Going higher than 160 hurts: at 200 the rooms went blurry and lost their props.
  • "One single short strip of tape" gives readable broken states; use it for 64-px rooms by default.
