The eleven new rooms

For each room: the blockout (step 1), the layout render, which is also the shipping on (step 2, 1 generation), then off and broken (step 4, edit tool, batched by width) and on with figures. There is no step 3 in this batch.

Findings

  • Skipping step 3 works. Every layout could ship as the on render as it came out. The rooms are cleaner and less grimy than the v13 inpainted masters.
  • Batched state edits hold up. Four 128-px rooms or three 64-px rooms per call, and every state stayed in register (difference from on 11–20 for broken). No white edge columns this time.
  • School: the first layout (strength 70) came back with receding floorboards and angled walls. At strength 85 with "stage set" wording it is flatter, but the floor still reads slightly as a plane. It is the weakest room of the batch.
  • Canteen: the first 3-slot layout had a door where the kitchen belongs, and little on the counter. The retry at strength 55 has the range, hood, counter and a communal table. It also gained some fake lettering on the menu board and a graffiti tag, which can be inpainted out if unwanted.
  • Floor lines: 88–93. The archive (92), security post (93) and battery bank (91) are outside the 86–90 tolerance. They are recorded in meta.json and work with floorY, but the tolerance should either widen to 86–93 or those rooms get a re-roll.
  • Sparse rooms: the clinic and superior suite read clearly, but a lot of wall is empty. That leaves room for figures, but it is less dense than the v13 machine rooms.
  • Oxygen garden: the moss wall behind a frame reads a little like a window. Worth checking against the no-exterior rule.
