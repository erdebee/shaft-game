The last sixteen rooms

Every building in the catalog now has an Eastward-style render with on, off and broken states: 36 of 36. These sixteen use the same detail recipe as v16 and v17. The shaft below shows all of them together at the adopted width: the 128-px stairwell plus ten build slots.

2–3 slot rooms (protein vats, modest suite, machine shop, recycler, luxury suite): a usable render in the first pair of tries at strength 75.

1-slot rooms (eleven of them): a first pass at strength 120 and 150 held flat for six; the rest came back as a box seen from the front. A second pass at 135 and 165, with the back wall described as "one flat plane filling the whole room edge to edge, no alcove, no inset box", fixed all four that were left.

Tried and dropped: rendering two 1-slot rooms side by side on one 128-px canvas and cutting it in half. The model ignores the centre line, so the rooms do not split cleanly.

Cost: 45 layout tries + 10 batched edit calls = 242 generations (~15 per building).

Redone on request: dumbwaiter, junction, purifier and cistern sat inside a brick or stone surround. 10 more tries plus 2 edit calls (~50 generations); the first versions are kept below each room.

Findings

  • 1-slot rooms turn into boxes because the model reads a tall, narrow frame as a doorway or alcove. Saying so explicitly ("one flat plane … no alcove, no inset box") plus strength 135–165 fixed every one that failed. Recommended default for 64-px rooms.
  • Guide colour clash: the palette's "slate" is the same colour as the guide's back wall, so shapes painted in it vanish from the guide (the cell bars were invisible at first). The new blockouts avoid it.
  • Holding cells is the plainest room (12 colours); C is a richer alternative with a slightly deeper floor.
  • Freight elevator and dumbwaiter were drawn as ordinary 1-slot rooms (a lift car in its shaft, a serving hatch). Whether they stay in the game is still open.
