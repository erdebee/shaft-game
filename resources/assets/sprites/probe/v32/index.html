Working figures

Round 32. Work loops for every staffed role, two seated poses, and the rooms staged with them. All clips are PixelLab animate_character v3 custom actions on the figures accepted in probe/v24 (east only, 1 generation each). Each is held to its first frame's palette and despeckled by build.py, which also installs them.

What changed in the game. Staff no longer stand still at fixed spots against the walls. Each works in a lane of the room playing their work loop, and every 22–44 seconds (sim time) walks to another spot in that lane. The school seats children at its three desks while it runs, with the teacher at the board. The auditorium seats an audience on its stools, seen from behind, with one speaker at the lectern. The miner shovels at the smelter and sorts scrap at the recycler and salvage post instead of swinging a pickaxe. Round 2: the grower (hydroponics, vats), kitchen hands, medic, engineer, pump tech and the miner at the smelter, recycler and salvage post now work seen from behind, facing the machine, tank or counter they work at; they still walk in profile. The grower keeps the side-on watering in the grove. Staging is data in the manifest (figures.rooms), logic in src/ui/view/crew.js. The scenes below run that same module.

1 · Work loops

Left: the idle loop the room showed until now. Right: the new work loop. 4×. The back views were generated from each figure's north rotation. The miner's two were pinned to a start and end frame so they close: for the shovel, a crouched frame holding the shovel; for sorting, his back with the pickaxe erased by hand.

2 · Seated poses

A generated key frame plus a one-pixel breath, 2 frames. The child faces the room and sits behind a desk; the resident faces the auditorium screen. The generator drew the resident's own stool, which is cut away at the seat row so the auditorium's stools (in its foreground cut) take its place.

3 · Staged rooms

Clock

Auditorium. The audience is the auditorium's 14 stools times the share of the labour pool with no shift (population.labour). At the opening that is about 45%, so 6 people. Full employment leaves only the speaker.
