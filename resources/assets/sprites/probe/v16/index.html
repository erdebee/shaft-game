Richer rooms — the eleven v14 rooms redone

You said the v14 rooms were too plain: simple backgrounds, a flat look, little colour, light or shadow, and no dithering. Each of the eleven is now redone with the detail recipe from the v15 test: a lit, dithered guide (the same blockout, redrawn in four lighting bands with dithering between them) at strength 75, with detailed shading and highly detailed as settings. The canteen and superior suite reuse the two v15 renders you've already seen. The nine rooms saved from v13 are unchanged.

Result. Lit rooms now use 22–50 colours (v14: 8–16) with visible dithering in light pools, textured back walls and many more props. Off and broken keep the detail. The off states' colour counts stay at 19–60.

Two tries per room, so there was a choice each time. Three rooms needed more: the clinic drew a window twice, then came back windowless with a stricter prompt; the school drew a receding plank floor twice, then held flat at strength 90; archive try B went into perspective.

The pasting problem is solved for the states. Edits now take the layout by its PixelLab URL rather than a pasted image string. Every guide upload also decoded this round.

Findings

  • The lit guide works across all room types. Machines, homes, gardens and offices all came back rich and still flat.
  • States keep the richness. The edit tool does not flatten the palette, and every state stays in register with its on render.
  • Pale rectangles become windows. The clinic guide's light cabinet block was read as a window twice. Naming the cabinet explicitly and adding "windowless, no window frame, no glass pane" fixed it. Guides should avoid large pale blocks high on the back wall.
  • 64-px rooms slide into perspective more easily. The school needed strength 90. Security post A also shows a slight tiled floor plane.
  • 64-px broken states are overloaded: warning tape criss-crosses the whole room. A lighter prompt ("one strip of tape") would suit single slots better.
  • Side walls read clearly on some rooms (workshop, clinic, scrubber bank) and only faintly on others (oxygen garden, canteen).
  • Now uneven with the saved nine. The v13 rooms look plainer next to these. Redoing them would cost roughly 180–200 generations.
