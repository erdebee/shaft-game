First ten rooms — the new ruleset, end to end

Ten catalog buildings made with the adopted workflow (spec §2.4): blockout → layout → inpainted master → state renders. Eastward style, no palette forcing, no people in the rooms, figures added from the figure layer. Every building has on, off and broken.

Zoom State · ShaftRoomsFindingsCost

What worked. All ten rooms are flat side views with their floor on rows 88–92, no people in them, and all 20 state renders stay in register with their master. Dropping the palette helped: the renders are richer and read as one set anyway. States made with the edit tool are convincing: off rooms are genuinely dark (brightness 22–43 vs 66–103 lit), and broken rooms show steam, scorch, tape and debris on an unchanged room.

What changed from the plan. (1) Inpainting over the approved layout does not keep the layout: it redraws the room from the description. (2) Image-to-image states did not work: at strength 300 nothing changed, and at 150 the room was destroyed. The edit tool replaced it. See Findings.

Cost: 458 generations for ten buildings (about 46 each). 1,332 left this cycle.

The ten rooms — every step

For each room: the blockout guide (step 1), the layout render (step 2, 1 generation), the inpainted master = on (step 3, ~20), off and broken (step 4, edit tool), and on with spec figures on the measured floor line. Captions give brightness and, for states, the mean difference from on.

Findings

  • No palette is better. Without forcing, colours stay in the Eastward family on their own (20–49 colours per room) and the rooms look richer. Keep it off.
  • Step 3 does not carry the layout. The generator, deep pump and hydroponics masters have different compositions from their approved layouts. Inpainting follows the text, not the pixels underneath. The masters also came back grimier and more textured. For the hydroponics bay and the canteen the layout render is arguably the better room.
  • States: use the edit tool. edit_image keeps the room and only changes light and damage. It batches up to four 128-px rooms per call for the same price, so states cost ~5–20 per room instead of 20 each. Image-to-image is rejected (kept in rejected/).
  • Canteen master came back with a floor plane (a pale floor receding to the back). It fails §2 and needs another master.
  • Simple suite floor is on row 92, outside the 86–90 tolerance, because the layout's floor band came out thin. It works with floorY recorded, but it should be tightened.
  • Hydroponics broken drifted more than the others (difference 47 vs 11–28): the whole wall darkened, not just the damage.
  • Presidential suite needed a second layout at strength 70: the first (strength 50) came back with a carpeted floor plane and angled furniture. The edit tool also left a 1-px white column on both edges of its off state; repaired by copying the neighbouring column (raw version in rejected/).
  • Figures hold one scale everywhere. The teal engineer still disappears against teal machinery, and councillors against dark wood.

Two savings are on the table: skip step 3 when the layout render is already good (saves ~20 per room: the hydroponics and canteen layouts, for instance), and batch states harder: all 128-px rooms four at a time for off and broken brings states to ~10 per room.
