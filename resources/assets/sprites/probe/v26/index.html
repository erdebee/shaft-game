Three new rooms — chosen

Picked: judicial B, exit C, and a 6-slot auditorium. All three now have their four state renders and are placed in the game — judicial on level 4, and the auditorium and the Exit on level 1, which was empty before. The rejected versions are kept below the finals, because what went wrong in them is what the blockouts were changed to avoid.

Auditorium — the wide window

You asked for the window centred in the back wall and at least 70% of the width. Five tries, all at 384 px (6 slots) in one pass. The trade-off that showed up: at 70%+ the window crowds everything else out — the generator keeps the panel and drops the chairs, the lectern, or both. The two that kept a full room below the window did it by putting the seating in perspective, which is the one thing §2 will not have.

The fix, if you like one of the windows but want its furniture back, is the same repair that worked on the last auditorium: keep the render and inpaint_image the chairs and lectern into the band below the ledge. Say which window and I will do that rather than keep rolling seeds.

The auditorium below is the earlier version, with the narrow window on the right — kept so there is something to compare the five wide ones against. It took three passes. At 6 slots it is 384 px, which pixflux will draw in one go, so there is no seam. Seed 26031 gave the hall but painted two people into it and turned the window into a literal porthole of blue glass; seed 26032 fixed the picture and lost the stage. Rather than keep rolling, 26031 was repaired with two inpaint_image passes — one over the porthole, which produced the bleached plain and the dead tree, and one over a two-rectangle mask covering both figures. That is the repair use the workflow's step 3 was kept for.

One thing to decide: 6 + 3 = 9 of level 1's 10 slots, so there is a one-slot gap of bare rock between the hall and the Exit. A security post fits it exactly, if it should be filled at all.

Auditorium — 7 slots, fixed, top level

448 px is wider than pixflux will draw (400 px per side), so the hall is generated as two 224-px halves off one 448-px guide and joined. The join is not finished — it needs an inpaint pass over the seam, and the two halves still disagree about floor height and chair size.

The right half is the keeper either way: the framed panel showing a bare dead tree on a bleached plain, with a brass frame and a bench beneath it. It is a screen, but the room is built so you forget that — which is why the prompt calls it a painted viewing panel and never says screen, sky or window (those words are banned from room prompts, spec §5).

What is still open
